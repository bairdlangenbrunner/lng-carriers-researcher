Overnight update: what the run found, and what needs a decision

An automated overnight pass checked the tracker backend (1,220 rows: 822 active, 364 on order, 34 proposed) against yard orderbooks, Korean disclosures, trade press and ship registries. Every line is a candidate waiting for human review, and every cited URL was checked to confirm the live page actually states the value it supports.

Mostly ships broadcasting on AIS under a real name while the registry still says on order. A press follow-up confirmed two, found three on sea trials and two not delivered; the rest are unresolved. The sanctioned Zvezda / Arctic LNG 2 hulls were deliberately left untouched.

Decisions waiting on a person

  1. Three duplicate placeholders. Woodside placeholder rows 1204–1206 duplicate the Seapeak × Samsung order already on rows 1165–1167: recommend deleting. The remaining Woodside rows, Equinor 4 and all 17 Mozambique LNG slots stay "proposed". TotalEnergies pushed the Mozambique slot deadline to September 2026, so re-check next month. July press also splits those 17 slots differently from the backend (Samho 9: MOL 5, K Line 4; Samsung 8: NYK 4, Maran Gas 4).
  2. Two likely duplicate pairs at Hanwha Philly: rows 1083 ↔ 1085 and 1132 ↔ 1086.
  3. How to cite "conventional". Vessel type is the tracker's own classification, never wording on a source page, so the citation check can never pass it. Either let a capacity-derived type stand on the capacity reference, or leave it uncited.
  4. Price convention. The backend mixes 250 + $m with 250000000 + USD. New fills use full USD.
  5. "Greenenergy" ship names look wrong against both the registry and AIS, which say "Greenergy".
  6. BW LNG capacity. Rows 1168/1169 are recorded as 174,000 cbm; PortNews, citing BW's fleet list, says 177,000 cbm on a new three-tank design.
  7. MISC FSRU price. Row 1162 says USD 330M; Businesskorea reports 484.8bn won (about USD 354M) for the same order.
  8. Possible mis-citations. The data-fill research flagged about 200 places where a source disagrees with an existing backend value. These are never applied automatically; they are listed for review in the workbook.
  9. The 45 unsettled delivery rows above.

Limits of this run

  • Coverage is thinner than a normal run. The web-search budget ran out part-way, one AIS site throttled requests all night (it is cited nowhere), and TradeWinds and Upstream paywalls hide many contract dates and prices.
  • Registry data (shipvault) is treated as a single source, medium confidence, and is never used for owners: it had IMO typos and wrong owner tags.
  • Only on-order rows were researched for blanks. Blanks on active rows are untouched.
  • The sweep for dead or decayed references on existing rows is paused and is not part of this update.
